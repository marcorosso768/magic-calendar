## 🦄 Magic Calendar v1.5.0

School days and holidays at a glance, and PDFs of several months.

### ✨ What's new
- **School days and vacation days**: enter the first and last day of school and the holiday periods (Christmas, Carnival, Easter, or your own family holidays). School days and vacation days get a different background, also in the PDF. The colours can be changed in Settings
- From 1 September the app asks for the dates of the new school year, with suggested dates to correct
- **Bridge days** (a working day between a holiday and the weekend) are marked in the calendar
- **Patron saint's day**: choose your city in Settings → School and holidays, or enter the date by hand. It counts as a holiday
- **PDF of several months**: this month and the next two, the school year (September to June), the whole year or the months you choose, one month per page in a single PDF
- **Redesigned update window**: app icon on the left, a slim progress bar with the downloaded size and a Cancel button; **Skip This Version** stops the app from offering that version at launch
- A short guided tour shows the new features (Help → What's New in This Version)

### 🖥️ Platforms
- macOS 13 Ventura or later: Apple Silicon → `Magic-Calendar-1.5.0-mac-arm64.dmg`, Intel → `Magic-Calendar-1.5.0-mac-x64.dmg`
- Windows 10 or later → `Magic-Calendar-1.5.0-win-x64.exe`

### 📦 Notes
If you have version 1.1.0 or later, this update installs itself. The new update window appears from the next update onwards.

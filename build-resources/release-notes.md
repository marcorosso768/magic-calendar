## 🦄 Magic Calendar v1.3.0

New things to see at a glance, and a backup of all your data.

### ✨ What's new
- **Today** is highlighted, and a 📍 Today button in the sidebar brings you back to the current month
- **Past days** are dimmed, optionally with a tick ✓ (Settings → Past days; can be turned off)
- **Italian public holidays** are shown on the calendar and in the PDF, Easter and Easter Monday included (can be turned off)
- **Day counts**: next to each day type, how many days of that type there are in the month
- **Countdown** under the title: today's day type and how many days until the next one ("Siamo qui! 💜 in 3 days")
- **Backup**: export all calendars, week plans, settings and images to one file, and import it back (Settings, or the File menu). Before an import, a copy of the current data is saved

### 🎨 Icon
- On macOS 26 the app icon is dark in dark mode also when the app is closed, without the glass effect

### 🖥️ Platforms
- macOS 13 Ventura or later: Apple Silicon → `Magic-Calendar-1.3.0-mac-arm64.dmg`, Intel → `Magic-Calendar-1.3.0-mac-x64.dmg`
- Windows 10 or later → `Magic-Calendar-1.3.0-win-x64.exe`

### 📦 Notes
If you have version 1.1.0 or later, this update installs itself.

## 🦄 Magic Calendar v1.4.0

Change many days at once, undo anything, and new windows for updates and About.

### ✨ What's new
- **Select several days**: drag across the calendar, or click a day and Shift+click another, then pick the day type in the bar at the bottom. A single click still opens the day
- **Weekly pattern** (🔁 in the sidebar): a type for each day of the week, also with alternate weeks (A, B, A, B…), applied to the month on screen or to a period of up to 400 days. A preview shows how many days will change
- **Undo and Redo** (⌘Z / ⇧⌘Z, Edit menu) for every change to the days: a single day, a selection, a pattern
- Images and notes are kept when a selection or a pattern changes the type of a day
- **New update window**: checking, "You're up to date", what's new in the update, download progress and restart, all in one small window
- **New About window** with version, project links and credits
- **What's new tour**: after an update, a short guided tour highlights the new features. Follow it or skip it; reopen it any time from Help → What's New in This Version

### 🖥️ Platforms
- macOS 13 Ventura or later: Apple Silicon → `Magic-Calendar-1.4.0-mac-arm64.dmg`, Intel → `Magic-Calendar-1.4.0-mac-x64.dmg`
- Windows 10 or later → `Magic-Calendar-1.4.0-win-x64.exe`

### 📦 Notes
If you have version 1.1.0 or later, this update installs itself.

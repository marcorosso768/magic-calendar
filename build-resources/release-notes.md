## 🦄 Magic Calendar v1.2.0

An update under the hood: a much newer engine, for a faster and safer app.

### ✨ What's new
- The app now runs on Electron 44 (it was Electron 28): two years of security fixes and performance improvements
- Up-to-date support for recent versions of macOS and Windows

### 🖥️ Platforms
- macOS 13 Ventura or later: Apple Silicon → `Magic-Calendar-1.2.0-mac-arm64.dmg`, Intel → `Magic-Calendar-1.2.0-mac-x64.dmg`
- Windows 10 or later → `Magic-Calendar-1.2.0-win-x64.exe`

### 📦 Notes
If you have version 1.1.0 or later, this update installs itself.
On macOS 12 or earlier the app stays on version 1.1.1 and does not offer this update.

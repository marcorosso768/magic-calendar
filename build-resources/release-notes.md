## 🦄 Magic Calendar v1.7.0

Import the school calendar from a file.

### ✨ What's new
- **Import a school calendar (.ics)**: if your school or region publishes its calendar as an .ics file, open it from the school-year window (📥 Import from .ics file). The app takes the first and last day of lessons and shows the events of the school year: holidays and closures are already ticked, meetings and other entries are left out. You choose what becomes a holiday
- Files exported by Magic Calendar itself can be imported too

### 🛠️ Under the hood
- Electron 44.10 (security and stability fixes), updated build and update tools

### 🖥️ Platforms
- macOS 13 Ventura or later: Apple Silicon → `Magic-Calendar-1.7.0-mac-arm64.dmg`, Intel → `Magic-Calendar-1.7.0-mac-x64.dmg`
- Windows 10 or later → `Magic-Calendar-1.7.0-win-x64.exe`

### 📦 Notes
If you have version 1.1.0 or later, this update installs itself.
